# 2026-11-01 — bf16 scalar-FMA Q4 prefill on the M4 (Mac mini, G16G)

Host: M4 Mac mini (Mac16,10, T8132, G16G GPU, 16 GB), Omarchy 4.0,
`linux-gravity-m4` 7.1.13, Vulkan Honeykrisp from techmore/mesa
`m4-honeykrisp-compute` (Apple9 compute port, guarded-load selects at
`172dceb`). No fp32 cooperative matrix on this driver. mlx-lm 0.31.3.
Wheel built from this branch on the host; A/B is within that one wheel
(`MLX_OMARCHY_NO_QMM_FMA_BF16=1` restores the tile route).

## Change

`shaders/qmm_fma.comp` gains a `-DUSE_BF16=1` build: bf16 x read through the
same uvec4 view (two exact shifts per word), bf16 scales and biases widened
exactly, RNE bf16 drain (the tile kernel's rounding and NaN rule). The f16
builds compile to byte-identical SPIR-V. `QuantizedMatmul` sends multi-row
bf16 4-bit/group-64 transposed matmuls to `QmmPrefillFmaBF16` (precise twin at
m >= 1024) wherever the f16 FMA route is reachable, that is, only on drivers
without the cooperative-matrix route. Coopmat drivers are unaffected.
